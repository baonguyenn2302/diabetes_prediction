5. CELL DANH GIA MO HINH
------------------------
Muc dich: Kiem tra mo hinh du doan dung hay sai va mo hinh phan biet hai lop
tot den dau. Day la cac bieu do danh gia (evaluation charts - bieu do danh gia).

confusion_matrix(y_test, y_pred)
- Tao ma tran gom True Negative, False Positive, False Negative, True Positive.
- y_test: nhan that; y_pred: nhan mo hinh du doan.

sns.heatmap(cm, annot=True, fmt='d', xticklabels=..., yticklabels=...)
- Hien thi confusion matrix dang bang mau.
- annot=True: hien so mau; fmt='d': hien so nguyen.
- truc X la Predicted; truc Y la Actual.
- Dung de xem mo hinh nham lan loai nao; dac biet quan trong khi bo sot ca benh.
- True Positive (TP - duong tinh that), False Positive (FP - duong tinh gia).
- True Negative (TN - am tinh that), False Negative (FN - am tinh gia/bo sot).

roc_curve(y_test, y_prob)
- Tinh cac diem cho duong ROC.
- ROC (Receiver Operating Characteristic - dac tinh van hanh bo thu) danh gia
  mo hinh o nhieu nguong phan loai khac nhau.
- Tra ve fpr (False Positive Rate), tpr (True Positive Rate) va nguong.
- y_prob phai la xac suat lop 1, thuong lay tu predict_proba()[:, 1].

axes[1].plot(fpr, tpr, lw=2, label=...)
- Ve duong ROC tu fpr va tpr.
- Duong cong cang gan goc tren-trai thi mo hinh cang tot.
- Truc X: FPR (False Positive Rate - ty le duong tinh gia).
- Truc Y: TPR (True Positive Rate - ty le duong tinh that/Recall - do nhay).

axes[1].plot([0, 1], [0, 1], linestyle='--')
- Ve duong ngau nhien de lam moc so sanh.

axes[1].fill_between(fpr, tpr, alpha=0.1)
- To mau nhat phia duoi duong ROC de de quan sat.

ROC-AUC (Area Under the Curve - dien tich duoi duong cong)
- La dien tich duoi duong ROC; cang gan 1 thi kha nang phan biet hai lop cang tot.
- AUC gan 0.5 gan voi du doan ngau nhien.
